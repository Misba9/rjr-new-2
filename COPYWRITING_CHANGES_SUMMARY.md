## 💡 CONVERSION COPYWRITING FORMULA USED

### For Each Section:
```
1. HOOK (Emotional/Problem-Based)
   ↓
2. BRIDGE (This is the problem everyone faces)
   ↓
3. SOLUTION (Here's what we do)
   ↓
4. SOCIAL PROOF (Here's proof it works)
   ↓
5. CTA (Here's what to do now)
```

### Example: Balcony Nets Service Card
```
1. HOOK: "Stop worrying about your family's safety"
2. BRIDGE: (implied - kids on balcony = parent worry)
3. SOLUTION: "Invisible balcony nets let kids play freely"
4. PROOF: "Trusted by 1000+ Bangalore families"
5. CTA: [Click → Learn More]
```
